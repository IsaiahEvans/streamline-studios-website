ABOUT

Isaiah Evans

I'm Isaiah — the photographer behind Streamline Studios. I create bold, clean imagery that feels real, cinematic, and intentional. I work with individuals, couples, brands, and automotive clients who want visuals that stand out without feeling over-posed or forced. Whether it's a graduation, event, brand shoot, or car feature, my goal is simple: guide you through the process, keep it stress-free, and deliver visuals you're proud to share.

I actually picked up my first camera for a Twitch facecam, not for photography. Once I had it in my hands, I started snapping pics for fun on the side — and that side thing led me straight into Charlotte's car meet scene. Something about the energy at those meets, the way people take pride in their builds, pulled me in fast. What started as a random idea turned into Streamline Studios, built shoot by shoot alongside the same community that got me hooked in the first place.

How I Work

Every shoot starts with understanding what you want and how you plan to use your images. I help with posing, locations, and direction so you never feel awkward or unsure. Turnaround is fast, communication is clear, and the final gallery is delivered ready for social media, web, or print.
